48-hour timelock

PoshEscrow, FMNYRewards and FMNYSale are owned by a TimelockController. Any admin change has to be scheduled in public and wait at least 48 hours before it can run, so users can see it coming. The deployer key holds no roles, and the distributor, passport and proof vault have no owner at all.

How funds are protected

  • Escrowed funds can only go to the seller on release, back to the buyer on refund, or as an arbiter rules in a dispute.
  • If an arbiter stays silent for 30 days, the pre-dispute outcome applies, so funds can't be stuck.
  • The app asks for exact approval amounts, never unlimited, and shows a plain-English review before your wallet opens. You can check and revoke the escrow's token approval on the Safety tab.
  • Only a hash of the deal terms goes on-chain. Email and personal details never do.

Code and infrastructure

  • Open source: github.com/statxqo-CLICKit/poshescrow, with secret scanning, push protection and Dependabot alerts on.
  • Served over HTTPS by Cloudflare with security headers (no framing, no MIME sniffing, strict referrer policy).
  • Machine-readable contact: /.well-known/security.txt
