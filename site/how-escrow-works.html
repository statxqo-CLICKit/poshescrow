How escrow protects buyers

Testnet demo only. POSH runs on Base Sepolia with free test tokens that have no real value. Contracts are unaudited. Not financial advice and not an offer to sell tokens.

The problem

Buying from a stranger online or on a marketplace means someone has to trust first. Pay first and the seller can vanish. Ship first and the buyer can vanish. Most peer-to-peer scams live in that gap.

What escrow changes

  • Your money waits in a smart contract, not in the seller's wallet. Nobody, including POSH, can quietly take it.
  • The seller is paid only when you release. You release once the item is in your hands.
  • No delivery by the deadline means a refund. You set the deadline (2 to 720 hours).
  • Disputes go to an arbiter, the default one or an approved arbiter you pick. If nobody rules within 30 days, the pre-dispute outcome applies, so funds never get stuck.
  • The deal terms are fingerprinted on-chain. Only a hash goes on-chain, so the agreement can't be rewritten later and your details stay private.
  • Trust Passport shows each wallet's on-chain deal history before you trade.

In-person deals: Handoff Mode

Buying a bike, a bag or a console at a meetup? Fund before you meet, verify the item on the spot, and release with one scan.

  1. Check the seller's Trust Passport.
  2. Fund the handoff and set a meetup window (2 to 24 hours).
  3. The seller shows a Handoff QR. You scan it and the app checks the item's Proof Vault fingerprint matches the listing.
  4. The seller taps Mark handed over. You then have 24 hours to release or dispute.
  5. Release pays the seller and moves the Proof Vault record to you in one step.

Demo walkthrough (about 5 minutes, free)

  1. Install MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet and switch to the Base Sepolia test network (the POSH site offers to add it).
  2. Get free test ETH from the Coinbase CDP faucet. It costs nothing and has no value.
  3. Open poshescrow.pages.dev and connect your wallet.
  4. On Deals, paste a second wallet of yours as the seller, type the terms (for example "Test: used bike, pickup Saturday"), enter 0.001 test ETH and a deadline, then tap Create deal.
  5. Review the plain-English summary, then confirm in your wallet. The deal now shows as funded.
  6. Release the payment to finish the deal, or wait out the deadline and claim a refund to see buyer protection work. Every finished deal gets a shareable Deal Certificate.
  7. Check the transaction on Basescan.

Stay safe

  • Never pay outside escrow, even for a discount.
  • Check every address character by character.
  • POSH never DMs you and never asks for your seed phrase.
  • Release only with the item in your hands. A release can't be undone.
