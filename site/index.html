01 — Protected deals

Escrow holds the money until the deal is done.

  1. Buyer funds the deal and a hash of the agreed terms goes on-chain with both wallets.
  2. Seller delivers, buyer releases payment, a small fee supports the protocol.
  3. No delivery by the deadline means a refund; disputes go to the default arbiter or an approved arbiter, or you settle together. An idle arbiter can be escalated, and if nobody rules within 30 days the pre-dispute outcome applies.
  4. Every finished deal earns a shareable 🏁 Deal Certificate.

02 — Handoff mode

Handoff Mode: escrow for in-person meetups.

Buying a bike, a designer bag or a console from a stranger? Fund before you meet, verify the item on the spot, and release with one scan.

  1. Check their Trust Passport.
  2. Buyer funds and sets a meetup window (2–24 h).
  3. Seller shows a Handoff QR; buyer scans and the app checks the item's Proof Vault fingerprint matches the listing.
  4. At the meetup the seller taps Mark handed over. The buyer then has 24 hours to release or dispute; after that the seller can claim.
  5. Release pays the seller AND transfers the Proof Vault filing to the buyer in one step. The buyer can take the money back only if the seller never marked it handed over before the window closed.

05 — Rewards

Rewards

FMNY is the reward token for using the platform. When an undisputed deal is released, buyer and seller share a rebate worth up to 25% of the fee at the pre-sale price, from a limited pool. Rewards can change or end.

  • Weekly unlock: what you earn this week becomes claimable when the week ends.
  • Both wallets need a POSH profile at least 3 days old.
  • Fee-bounded caps: a reward is never worth more than the fee behind it (at most 50%, set at 25%), up to 1,000 FMNY per wallet per day and 50,000 lifetime, one reward per buyer–seller pair per week.
  • A fixed weekly budget of 180,000 FMNY that halves every two years; at most 37.44M FMNY can ever be emitted.

07 — Safety

Stay safe: the scams escrow stops, and how to spot the rest.

  • Never pay outside escrow, even for a discount.
  • Check every address character by character; scammers swap look-alikes.
  • POSH never DMs you or asks for your seed phrase.
  • Release only with the item in your hands; a release can't be undone.
  • New or low passport? Use Handoff Mode or a custom arbiter.

    • If MetaMask shows something different from the review, reject it.
    • Never share your seed phrase. No real support person will ask for it.
    • Use a hardware wallet or a multisig for any owner key that controls live funds.
